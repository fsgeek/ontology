## 4. The three falsifications (launched in parallel 2026-06-04)

Per PI direction: run all three together, because **the joint distribution of their results is the signal** — disagreement among them is what generates the next round of hypotheses. Each is framed as a falsification attempt; a break is a door, and "none falsify" is cause for alarm (go find the analysis error), not celebration.

- **#2 Silence-upstream (theory-join).** Hypothesis: silence-manufacture (governance) is UPSTREAM of granularity-collapse (ontology) — the `estar` layer is impossible to populate honestly without breaking manufactured silence first. Falsified if the ser/estar layers are populatable/non-contradictory under current (non-construction-provenance) conditions in the Olorin case. If it holds: the program is ONE causal line, and "necessary-not-sufficient" becomes "sufficient-given-silence-breaking."

- **#3 ZK-cost (build-move with a go/no-go gate).** Hypothesis: recursive-SNARK construction-provenance is sub-linear / cheaper than one comprehensive post-hoc audit. Falsified if proof cost is O(model-size) or worse. If it holds: the adoption argument inverts from ethics-pitch to "commitment pays for itself."

- **#1 Forced-grounding (experiment + architecture).** Hypothesis: separability does NOT protect against parametric leak — a separable FNMA substrate whose weights encode race-predictive pretraining correlations passes the separability audit while failing fair-lending. Falsified if a forced-grounded and a parametric-leak model with identical band-level predicate-satisfaction produce identical disparate-impact profiles. If it holds: it KILLS the keystone headline and reframes the design obligation to "architect forced grounding (which layer is the decision made in?)."

**Results (2026-06-04, `research-note-three-falsifications-joint-2026-06-04.md`):** #1 **HELD** (separability blind to parametric leak — but contaminated: it flatters the author's own forced-grounding construct, and its FNMA-specific kill is unearned because FICO/DTI/LTV are largely fixed bureau pulls, not reshadeable scalars; claim the principle, not the FNMA kill). #2 **INDETERMINATE — and now BLOCKED:** the deciding estar-population experiment requires Olorin data, and **there is no Olorin data** (confirmed with Tony, 2026-06-04). One-causal-line-vs-two is an open question the project **cannot currently decide**; it is not a queued experiment. #3 **FALSIFIED twice** (ZK super-linear + proves provenance≠fairness). Net: contribution is now a two-axis impossibility result (allocation axis closeable by separability; parametric/curation/silence axis not cheaply closeable), smaller-headline and more reviewer-robust than the one-axis sufficiency claim it replaces. The only **runnable** open door is H-A (does forced grounding eliminate the leak or relocate it to retrieval-set curation) — constructive, no substrate needed.
